

A = [[1, 2], [0, 1]]

B = [[2, 0], [3, 1]]

1. Calculate AB

AB = [[1×2+2×3, 1×0+2×1], [0×2+1×3, 0×0+1×1]]

AB = [[8, 2], [3, 1]]

2. Calculate BA

BA = [[2×1+0×0, 2×2+0×1], [3×1+1×0, 3×2+1×1]]

BA = [[2, 4], [3, 7]]

Therefore:

AB ≠ BA

Explanation: Matrix multiplication is not generally commutative. Changing the order of the matrices can produce a different result.
