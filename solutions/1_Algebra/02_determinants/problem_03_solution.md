Exercise 3. Swapping Two Rows

Given:

A=\begin{pmatrix}
1&2&0\\
0&3&1\\
2&1&1
\end{pmatrix}

First, calculate the determinant:

\det(A)=1(3\cdot1-1\cdot1)
-2(0\cdot1-1\cdot2)

=2+4

\boxed{\det(A)=6}

Now swap the first and second rows:

B=\begin{pmatrix}
0&3&1\\
1&2&0\\
2&1&1
\end{pmatrix}

Swapping two rows changes the sign of the determinant.

Therefore:

\det(B)=-\det(A)

\boxed{\det(B)=-6}

The calculation confirms this result.
