# Determinants – Exercise 3

## Exercise 3. Swapping Two Rows

Given:

$$A=\begin{pmatrix}1&2&0\\0&3&1\\2&1&1\end{pmatrix}$$

### Step 1: Calculate the determinant of A.

$$\det(A)=1(3\cdot1-1\cdot1)-2(0\cdot1-1\cdot2)$$ $$=1(2)-2(-2)=2+4$$
$$\boxed{\det(A)=6}$$

### Step 2: Swap the first and second rows.

$$B=\begin{pmatrix}0&3&1\\1&2&0\\2&1&1\end{pmatrix}$$

### Step 3: Predict the new determinant.

Swapping two rows changes the sign of the determinant.

$$\det(B)=-\det(A)=-6$$

### Step 4: Verify by direct calculation.

$$\det(B)=0-3(1\cdot1-0\cdot2)+1(1\cdot1-2\cdot2)$$ $$=-3+(1-4)=-3-3$$
$$\boxed{\det(B)=-6}$$
