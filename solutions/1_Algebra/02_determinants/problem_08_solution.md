# Determinants – Exercise 8

## Exercise 8. Determinant of a Product

Given:

$$\det(A)=3,\qquad\det(B)=-5$$

### 1. Calculate $\det(AB)$.

$$\det(AB)=\det(A)\det(B)=3\cdot(-5)=\boxed{-15}$$

### 2. Calculate $\det(BA)$.

$$\det(BA)=\det(B)\det(A)=(-5)\cdot3=\boxed{-15}$$

The two determinants are equal because multiplication of real numbers is
commutative. In general, $AB\neq BA$, but $\det(AB)=\det(BA)$.

### 3. Calculate $\det(A^{-1}B)$.

Since $\det(A)=3\neq0$, the inverse of A exists.

$$\det(A^{-1})=\frac{1}{\det(A)}=\frac13$$

$$\det(A^{-1}B)=\det(A^{-1})\det(B)=\frac13\cdot(-5)$$

$$\boxed{\det(A^{-1}B)=-\frac53}$$
